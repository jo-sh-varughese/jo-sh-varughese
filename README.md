<details>
<summary><b>🔎 Expand — one-line takeaways</b></summary>
<br/>

- **01 · E-Process Retrofit** — Adds a formal anytime-valid false-alarm guarantee to a training-integrity monitor; finds and fixes a composite-null validity bug, quantifies a severe validity–power trade-off, and derives a reweighted betting mixture that Pareto-dominates every other fix tried (MNIST, CIFAR-10 / ResNet-18).
- **02 · SSIT** — Proves sequential position/verbosity debiasing leaves a residual when the biases interact; introduces a label-free swap-symmetry test that recovers the interaction with no gold labels. Positive interaction found in 6 of 7 open judges.
- **03 · Certified Delta Attribution** — On a real GPT-2 SAE, manifold alignment (not norm) predicts decomposition fidelity under steering — inverting the intuitive safety ordering. CDA preserves **100%** of the steering effect vs ~30% for projection-based corrections.
- **04 · CN-FedProto** — Independently-noised per-round DP for prototype FL is mis-accounted: a curious server can average releases to recover a client's prototype. Proposes a correlated-noise, participation-bounded release, and openly reports a calibration error caught in the first analysis.
- **05 · CIFAR-10 Gap Audit** — Eight-mechanism, Bonferroni-corrected audit of a published Byzantine-robust DP-FL algorithm; the recovered gap holds only in the no-DP IID setting — and the paper says so plainly.
- **06 · MCR²-KL** — Folds four corrective mechanisms into one loop to fix five failure modes of mode-seeking reverse-KL VI, with full ablation attributing each gain.
- **07 · Trajectory Auditing** — Optimizer-trajectory curvature as a black-box integrity signal; catches a harness bug where a "perfect AUROC" baseline never crossed its own alert threshold, and reports honestly where the method fails to transfer.
